# SANDWERK — running order

12 tracks, 74:58 total. This is the **story arc**, not the full 19-script
catalogue: `tracks/dune/` also holds five seamless game-state loops
(`arrakis_winds_v3`, `spice_must_flow`, `stillsuit`, `sandstorm_coriolis`,
`base_under_attack`) and the pre-Dune test piece `ambient_track` — built to
underlie gameplay with no build and no ending, not to be listened to
start-to-finish. Excluded here the same way Unsung and Hammerwerk were
excluded from Maschinenherz.

Sequencing follows the story the scripts' own design notes already tell —
Theme A (the duduk line) is stated in Night Pursuit and resolved, at last,
in Sihaya's refrain; the mastering reset and new key in The Navigator is
explicitly inherited by every track after it; Kwisatz Haderach quotes
Water of Life / Sleeper Awakens / Fall of Arrakeen by name, so it can't
come before them.

Track titles include some of Frank Herbert's invented terms (Kwisatz
Haderach, Sihaya, Kanly, Arrakeen, Sardaukar) — low risk; titles aren't
copyrightable.
